Fortea 2024. Argues ε4/ε4 represents a distinct genetic form of AD with biomarker abnormality from age 55 and near-complete penetrance by 85. Framing is contested; NACC cohort over-represents symptomatic patients.
Context. Figures derive from cohorts of predominantly European ancestry. Risk is age-dependent. APOE is one of many genetic and environmental contributors; modifiable factors (hypertension, hearing loss, smoking, diabetes, social isolation) also shape lifetime risk. Genotype is probabilistic, not diagnostic.
