MMSE trajectory by treatment and dementia type

Illustrative curves parameterised from Knight 2018, Xu 2021, Xu 2024, Truzzi 2022. Baseline MMSE 22 for AD, 24 for DLB.

Untreated AD AChEI in AD AChEI + memantine, moderate AD AChEI in DLB Untreated DLB
Untreated AD declines about 2.5 MMSE points per year. AChEI produces an early symptomatic improvement of roughly 1 point, then parallel decline. The same drugs produce a larger and more durable benefit in DLB.

Curves on AChEi run roughly parallel to the untreated trajectory in AD, offset by ~1 MMSE point. The DLB response is meaningfully larger and more durable.
